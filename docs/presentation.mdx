## Introduction

We are an travel company focusing on the Đà Lạt region. Đà Lạt is a popular tourist attraction, however its popularity changes within a year, based on some factors:
 - Seasonal cycles (theo mùa).
 - Momentary pulses (tức thời).

An ad campaign that doesn't conform to these factors will risk not being able to connect with the demographic, ineffective. An example of a generic ad is like "20% discount this season!".

There are questions:
 1. What are the themes that yield most opportunity?
 2. Is the trend still fresh, or is it going down / the bubble is bursting?
 3. What sub-demographic does it align? Teenagers? Adults? Families?
 4. How to we handle risks and back-up plans?

## Business problem statement [^abc]

[^abc]: I also call it "problem refinement"

Who are we? We are an OTA (Online Travel Agency) startup, competing in the same space as Airbnb, Traveloka, Agoda, TripAdvisor.

Who are our demographic? Travelers who use apps to book hotels and plan trips etc, use our app and competitors. Especially travelers of the Đà Lạt region which is our specialty, strength.

What's the problem? Our marketing team is making an ad campaign, and needs a theme that best connects with the demographic now. Since it is nearing December, the theme should be related to winter / christmas (but of course, more specific than that).

What does the team need? A recommendation in the form of a set (1-3) of specific theme keywords + general visualization of statistics.

## Decision makers

We know that the main decision maker is the marketing manager. They will decide the theme, target demographic, time window.

We also know other roles with influence:
 - Content strategist. They will decide the text and illustrations based on the themes and theme keywords.
 - Partner coordinator. They will inform partner hotels of the theme which they can adapt to.

## System architecture

It is a (classicial) two-level architecture, an ETL pipeline

Why a two-level architecture and not an independent data mart architecture, or a lakehouse?

A single data warehouse at the storage area means that all front-end tools access from one centralized source that is the storage. Centralization can be troublesome. It is less project-oriented. However, it is simple, good for now. (Also I can't think of good contextual reasons to make it more robust). (reference: slides CO4031 DW-DSS - Chapter 2 - DW-Basic Issues, page 7, adapted from Strange (1997))

Our architecture has four layers:

1. Source data systems
2. Data staging area
3. Data storage area
4. Front-end
